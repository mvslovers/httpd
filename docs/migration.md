# Migrating from HTTPD 3.x

The step from HTTPD 3.x to 4.x, and from one 4.x release to the next, is
described in the _HTTPD User's Guide_ (ML07-0001), chapter "Upgrading HTTPD":
the sections "Upgrading from HTTPD 3.x" and "Upgrading from HTTPD 4.0.x" say
what is gone, what has a new form and what to convert. The keywords of the
Parmlib member that replaced the Lua configuration are in the _HTTPD Reference_
(ML07-0002), "The HTTPPRM Keywords".

The manuals are attached to each release from 4.5.0 on as
`ml07-0001-<edition>.pdf` and `ml07-0002-<edition>.pdf`, and their sources are
in [`docs/books`](books).
